Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Where can I buy quality furniture in Kenya?

PeachBay Furniture is a furniture and interior-design business on Nyali Road, Mombasa. You can visit Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm, browse the catalogue on this page, or message us on WhatsApp on +254 748 333 486.

Does PeachBay Furniture offer new and refurbished furniture?

Yes. PeachBay offers both new and refurbished furniture for homes and offices.

Does PeachBay offer interior design services?

Yes. Services include interior design consultation, interior design quotations and house plans, alongside lighting, wallpaper, décor and furniture installation.

How do I book an interior design consultation?

Use the consultation form on this page with your preferred date and time. It opens WhatsApp with your request ready to send, and we confirm the appointment there.

Can PeachBay make custom furniture?

Yes. Use the custom furniture quotation form to tell us the furniture type, dimensions, material, finish and quantity. We review it and reply with a quotation.

How much does furniture cost at PeachBay?

Some pieces show a listed price on this page. For everything else, price depends on size, materials, finish and quantity, so we give a quotation on request. Use the quotation form or WhatsApp us.

Does PeachBay deliver, and do you install furniture?

Yes. PeachBay delivers furniture across Kenya and offers furniture fixing and installation. Delivery cost depends on your location and the size of the order, so ask us for a quote.

Does PeachBay offer wallpaper installation?

Yes. PeachBay supplies wallpapers and offers wallpaper installation as part of its interior finishing services.

What furniture and décor products are available?

New and refurbished home and office furniture, plus home décor, decorative accessories, lighting, artificial plants, cement pots and wallpapers.

Do you supply furniture for offices and institutions?

Yes. PeachBay handles office furniture orders. Send us the quantities and requirements through the corporate enquiry form for a bulk quotation.

How can I contact PeachBay Furniture?

Call or WhatsApp +254 748 333 486, email ombokfelistus@gmail.com, or visit Nyali Road, Mombasa. We are on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook as peachbay56.
